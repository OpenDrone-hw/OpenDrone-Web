Preorders are open. See [the preorder page](/preorder) for the batches, prices,
funding targets and dates.

**Batch 1 ships in November.** While batch 1 lasts, OpenFC Lite and OpenESC
ship from Belgium to EU addresses in early November 2026.

**Everything else is a preorder.** You can order it for delivery in the EU, in
the United States (import duties included) and in the other countries offered
at checkout. Orders count toward a funding target that closes on 15 December
2026. If the target is reached, your order ships by 31 March 2027. If it is
not, you choose a refund or to keep waiting.

Pick your delivery country on the product page to see the price, shipping and
dates for your country. Outside the EU and the US, import charges may be
payable on delivery.

Preorders are paid in full. You can cancel before delivery; the
[preorder terms](/en/algemene-voorwaarden#art-7bis) explain refunds, missed
funding targets and orders with items from different batches.

Shops interested in stocking OpenDrone can [request a bulk quote](/wholesale).
