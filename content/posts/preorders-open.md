You can order every OpenDrone product in [the shop](/products) now. How it
works is on [the preorder page](/preorder); this is the short version.

## The stack ships in October

Batch 1 of the flight controller and the ESC, 250 of each board in each
size, is paid for and in production. It ships late October 2026 while it
lasts. After that the stack gets a funding target like every other product.

## Everything else has a funding target

The receivers, frames and motors are funded by preorders. Each product
option has a public target, set at the quantity we order from the supplier.
When a target is reached we place that order, and the product ships about
10 weeks later. Every target has a deadline of 31 December 2026. If a
product misses it, everyone who ordered it chooses: a refund, or keep the
order for the new ship date.

## What you pay

You pay in full when you order. Per product option, units 1 to 100 cost 20%
less than the retail price, units 101 to 250 cost 10% less, and from unit
251 the retail price applies.

Shipping is a flat rate per order: [every rate is on the shipping page](/en/shipping).
We do not collect import duties at checkout. Outside the EU, including the
United States, the carrier collects any import duties and taxes from you on
delivery.

## One order, one parcel

An order ships once, complete. If you order the stack together with a
product that is still funding, the whole order waits until everything in it
is ready. Order separately if you want the stack in October.
