Do not throw electrical parts, such as flight controllers, ESCs, receivers and motors, in household waste. The crossed-out wheeled bin on a product or its packaging means it must be collected separately.

Bring them to a municipal container park or recycling point, or to a shop that sells similar equipment. Larger shops take back small items under 25 cm free of charge, without a purchase. In Belgium, find a Recupel collection point at [recupel.be](https://www.recupel.be).

Electronics contain substances that can harm health and the environment when they are thrown away with household waste; separate collection lets them be treated and recycled safely.

Packaging: put the cardboard with paper and cardboard, and plastic packaging with PMD or plastic packaging, following your local rules.
