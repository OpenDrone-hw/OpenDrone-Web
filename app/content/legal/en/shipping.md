## Shipping and delivery

Incutec BV ships orders from Belgium to most European Union countries. The products are paid in full when the order is placed. Shipping is not charged at checkout: it is billed separately, per destination zone, once the complete order is ready to ship. The rate per zone is published on this page, so it is known before the order is placed.

The Dutch version (/shipping) is the legally authoritative text for consumers residing in Belgium. This English translation is informative only.

### Delivery time

Orders are normally shipped within 2 business days of payment receipt. The final delivery deadline is 30 calendar days after the order, unless explicitly agreed otherwise (Art. VI.43 of the Belgian Code of Economic Law, Art. 7 of our General Terms and Conditions). If Incutec BV fails to deliver within the 30-day deadline, the consumer may set a reasonable additional period. If Incutec BV still fails to deliver within that additional period, the consumer is entitled to terminate the contract free of charge (Art. VI.43 §2 WER), unless the original delivery deadline was essential or Incutec BV has already refused to deliver.

### Pre-orders

Some products are sold as pre-orders. They are marked as such, with their expected ship date, on the product page, in the cart and on the order confirmation. The full price of the products is charged when the order is placed; shipping is charged separately once the order is ready to ship, as described under "Shipping rates and countries" below. At launch, the flight controller and ESC ship from mid-October 2026; every other pre-order product ships in about 10 weeks after the order. The stated ship date is the delivery time agreed between the consumer and Incutec BV within the meaning of Art. VI.43 of the Belgian Code of Economic Law, so the 30-day default deadline above does not apply to pre-order items.

An order that combines in-stock and pre-order items ships as one parcel once every item is in stock: one delivery per order. If Incutec BV cannot meet the stated ship date, it informs the consumer and offers, at the consumer's choice, a new ship date or a full refund within 14 days.

The consumer may cancel a pre-order before shipment for a full refund. The 14-day right of withdrawal runs from delivery and can also be exercised at any time before delivery; Incutec BV refunds the full amount paid within 14 days of the cancellation or withdrawal notice.

Pre-order payments fund the production run and stock purchase for the order. They carry no interest and confer no equity, revenue share or other ownership interest in Incutec BV.

Parcels shipped outside the European Union may be subject to import duties and taxes payable by the consumer on delivery, unless the chosen delivery method states that duties are paid by Incutec BV.

### Funded pre-orders

Each hardware product has a funding target, stated publicly on its product page. If a product does not reach its funding target, the pre-orders for that product are refunded in full. In an order that combines several products, a product that has not reached its funding target is refunded and the remaining items ship together in the single delivery. Our [pre-order page](/preorder) explains the funding model.

### Shipping rates and countries

Shipping is not charged at checkout. Once every item in the order is ready to ship, Incutec BV invoices the shipping cost for the destination zone and sends the consumer a payment request by mail with a secure payment link. The order is dispatched once that shipping invoice is paid.

The rates below are per order, include VAT, and depend on the destination zone and the order value. The rate that applies to an order is the rate published on this page at the time the order is placed.

### If the shipping payment request is not paid

If the payment request for shipping is not paid, Incutec BV sends reminders. The order is held; it is not cancelled automatically. The consumer may cancel the order at any time before dispatch and receive a full refund of the products. This does not affect the right of withdrawal described above.

### Risk during shipping

The risk of damage or loss in transit lies with Incutec BV until the moment of delivery to the consumer (Art. VI.44 of the Belgian Code of Economic Law).

### Tracking

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive an email confirmation with a tracking number.
